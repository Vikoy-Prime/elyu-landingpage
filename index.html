About

San Juan Surf Beach is the heart of La Union's surfing culture. Known for its consistent waves, laid-back vibe, and stunning sunsets, it's a must-visit for both beginner and experienced surfers alike. The beach strip is lined with vibrant cafes, surf schools, and boutique accommodations.

Things to Do

Surfing lessons with certified local instructors

Watch the breathtaking West Philippine Sea sunset

Try local food & craft beer at beachside cafes

Explore nearby shops, murals, and coffee spots

Best Time to Visit

October to April (Ideal for surfing waves and sunny beach days)

About La Union

La Union is a coastal province located in the Ilocos Region of Luzon, Philippines. Known for its pristine beaches, world-class waves, majestic mountains, and warm, welcoming people, it's a place where nature, culture, and local life come together seamlessly.

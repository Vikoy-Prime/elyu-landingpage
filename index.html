Free Entry

Overview

San Juan is acclaimed as the Surfing Capital of Northern Luzon. The long sandy beach offers consistent wave swells suitable for first-time surfers as well as seasoned riders. Along the main strip, visitors will find a vibrant coastal community filled with specialty coffee shops, eco-friendly resorts, and fusion dining options.

San Fernando City Guide

Capital City of La Union — Heritage, Commerce, and Coastline

City Overview

San Fernando City serves as the administrative, financial, and educational center of the Ilocos Region. Perched on San Fernando Bay, it provides an exquisite mix of vibrant city life, cultural landmarks like Ma-Cho Temple, and coastal recreation spots around Poro Point Special Economic and Freeport Zone.
